# R1a1 — What the Science Actually Says

An evidence-based analysis of R1a1 haplogroup distribution across Indian caste and tribal populations.

## Features
- Caste-wise R1a1 frequency bar charts (24 populations)
- Detailed data table with 26 data points
- **Downloadable dataset** (CSV, JSON, TSV)
- 11 peer-reviewed research papers (expandable)
- 9 expert quotations from geneticists
- 8 news articles
- 13 full references

## Live Site
**https://alok-108.github.io/r1a1-science/**

## Data Sources
All data compiled from peer-reviewed Y-chromosome studies including Sharma et al. (2009), Sengupta et al. (2006), Kivisild et al. (2003), and others.

09 - Debunking the Myth

R1a1 Is NOT Exclusive to Upper Castes

A systematic review of the evidence against the claim that R1a1 is exclusive to Brahmins and Kshatriyas.

The Claim Being Examined
"R1a1 is found only in upper castes - Brahmins and Kshatriyas - and is absent or negligible in SC/ST, Jat, Gujjar, Yadav, and other communities."
This claim is used to argue that R1a1 is a genetic marker of Aryan ancestry and that caste hierarchy reflects a racial divide. Below is what the peer-reviewed evidence actually shows.
What the claim implies

R1a1 should be present only in upper castes. If true, it would support a racial interpretation of the caste hierarchy and suggest Brahmins and Kshatriyas are genetically distinct from other communities.

What the evidence shows

R1a1 is found across ALL major Indian communities - Muslim Gujjars (78%), Jats (55-65%), Yadavs (50%), Saharia tribal (50.9%), Kotas tribal (41%), Chenchu tribal (26%), Dalits (17.2%). No study states exclusivity.
